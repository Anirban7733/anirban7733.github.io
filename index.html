Anirban Nath

Ph.D. Student, Department of Statistics, Columbia University

I am a Ph.D. student in Statistics at Columbia University. Before coming to Columbia, I received my Bachelor of Statistics and Master of Statistics from the Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata.

My research focuses on uncertainty quantification and distribution-free inference, in particular conformal prediction for clustering, together with high-dimensional statistical inference and causal inference. In Summer 2026 I was a Data Science Intern at Capital One, working on causal inference methods for estimating heterogeneous treatment effects.

My research interests include:

  • Uncertainty quantification
  • Conformal prediction and distribution-free inference
  • High-dimensional statistical inference
  • Unsupervised learning and clustering
  • Causal inference
